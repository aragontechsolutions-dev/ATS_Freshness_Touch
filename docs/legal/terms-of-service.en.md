## 3. The travel deposit

When you book, we place a **hold of $35.00** on your card. A hold is not a
charge: the money is reserved by your bank but does not leave your account.

- It is **credited in full against your final invoice**. It is not an extra
  cost.
- If you cancel in time (section 5), it is **released**. Depending on your
  bank, it can take a few business days to disappear from your statement.
- A hold **expires on its own after 7 days at the latest**, which is the
  maximum the card networks allow. If it expires, the money returns to you
  without anyone doing anything.
- **We only ever charge it in one situation: you cancel after our team is
  already travelling to you.** At that point the drive is already paid for in
  fuel and wages.

Your booking is not confirmed until the hold is authorised. If your card is
declined, the appointment slot is not reserved and we will tell you so.

## 4. Paying the balance

The rest of the price — the total minus the deposit — is due on the day of the

you.

Tips are **never required and never added automatically**. If you want to tip,
it is entirely up to you.
